## Humanizer-zh editing guidance

`references/audience-copy.md` adapts selected editing principles from [op7418/Humanizer-zh](https://github.com/op7418/Humanizer-zh/blob/f4518a8eab97b8bfebc66a89d34320a89bef6930/SKILL.md), commit `f4518a8eab97b8bfebc66a89d34320a89bef6930` (retrieved 2026-10-30 UTC). The upstream file identifies its basis as blader/humanizer v3.0.0 and Humanizer-zh PR 39. The retrieved SKILL.md SHA-256 is `95627fd4437d886f98032ccc62d79136e6310f1fc04e354bb86431795ff206df`.

Adaptation scope: preserve facts and certainty, match the intended register, edit concrete expression problems rather than applying a word blacklist, retain valid exceptions, and compare the rewrite with its source. These principles are integrated into the existing reporting and audit steps. The external Skill's tool declarations, full pattern catalogue, examples and execution workflow are not installed. The teaching examples in audience-copy.md were written for this project with complete synthetic inputs.

The adapted method is self-contained in this package; the links above are provenance, not runtime reading dependencies. The original research archive is not required to use it. These notices describe source reuse, not a claim of tested model quality or endorsement by the original authors.
