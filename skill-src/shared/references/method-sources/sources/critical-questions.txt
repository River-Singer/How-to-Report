Yu and Zenker examine how critical questions can evaluate the premises, the connection from premises to a conclusion, and the conclusion itself. Their proposed general framework is an argumentation-theory contribution; it does not guarantee that a report's evidence is exhaustive or true. Use targeted questions to expose the missing inference or condition and stop when the next useful step is obtaining facts. Source: Shiyang Yu and Frank Zenker, *Schemes, Critical Questions, and Complete Argument Evaluation*, Argumentation 34, 469–498 (2020), https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10503-020-09512-4 .
