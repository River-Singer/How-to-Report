Separate the claim from its supporting information and the reasoning that connects them. Necessary qualifications and plausible objections can change how strongly a conclusion is stated. A filled diagram is not proof that its premise or bridge is sound. In reports, ask what makes an observed capability relevant to a business judgment and which conditions remain untested.
